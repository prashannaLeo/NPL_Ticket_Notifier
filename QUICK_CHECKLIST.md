## 📱 IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG

### No Alert Received?
1. Check phone volume is ON
2. Check Telegram notifications enabled
3. Send `/status` to bot - verify it's working
4. Manually check Khalti website for new tickets

### Khalti Site Slow?
1. Very normal - heavy traffic on ticket release
2. Don't refresh page repeatedly
3. Wait 10-20 seconds for page to load
4. Try Khalti app instead (faster backend)

### Payment Failed?
1. Check card has sufficient balance
2. Ensure card is not blocked or expired
3. Try your backup payment method
4. Wait 1-2 minutes and retry
5. Contact bank if recurring failures

### Payment Timed Out?
1. Check your bank account - you may already be charged
2. Refresh page to check order status
3. If not charged, tickets may be available still
4. Check email for confirmation
5. Don't retry immediately - wait 30 seconds

### OTP Not Received?
1. Check SMS inbox and spam folder
2. Ensure mobile network has signal
3. Ask bank to resend OTP
4. Wait up to 1 minute (sometimes slow)
5. If still nothing, cancel and retry with different card

### Still Didn't Get Tickets?
1. Don't worry - tickets restock 5-10 minutes later
2. Keep monitoring
3. Next batch will trigger alerts again
4. Try again with refreshed mind
5. Share the experience - misses happen to everyone!
